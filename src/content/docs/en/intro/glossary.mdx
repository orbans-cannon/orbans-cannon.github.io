The wording on this site follows this table.

A word with a dotted underline on a page shows its full English name and a one- or two-sentence definition when you hover over it or focus it with the keyboard; clicking it brings you back here.

:::note[About Chinese translations and the asterisk]
A Chinese translation marked with **\*** is the author's **reference translation**. These terms are usually just said in English or as an English abbreviation, with no fixed usage in Traditional Chinese, so the pages themselves still use the English; the Chinese appears only here and in the hover cards for reference. Terms that already have a fixed Chinese expression (for example 拜占庭容錯) carry no asterisk.
:::
