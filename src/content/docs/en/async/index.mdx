**Opening question**: FLP says determinism is impossible, so what exactly does randomization buy?

## Planned chapters

<PartRoute timing="async">

1. **After FLP**: the impossibility itself is in [Impossibility and Lower Bounds](/en/impossibility/async/flp/); this part starts from its boundary and asks which assumption can be relaxed to get around it.
2. **Ben-Or**: the prototype of getting around FLP with randomization, local coin and expected rounds.
3. **Bracha reliable broadcast (RBC)**: no signatures, echo / ready thresholds, proof of totality.
4. **Common coin**: the threshold-signature implementation of Cachin–Kursawe–Shoup, and the role of DKG.
5. **MMR ABA**: modern asynchronous BA with no signatures and $O(n^2)$ messages.
6. **VABA**: BA with external validity and expected $O(n^2)$ communication.
7. **AVID**: asynchronous verifiable information dispersal, the foundation of Narwhal-style designs.
8. **HoneyBadgerBFT**: from a single BA to practical asynchronous SMR.
