## Planned coverage

- **The lower bound without signatures**: <Cite id="fl1982" /> proves that at least $f+1$ rounds are needed.
- **Generalizing to any authentication**: <Cite id="ds1983" /> extends this lower bound to any form of authentication and any message type; the proof does not depend on a particular authentication protocol. This is the proof this chapter will study closely.
- **The matching upper bound**: Dolev–Strong give an algorithm with exactly $f+1$ rounds and $O(nf)$ messages. The algorithm itself is left to the synchrony part; this chapter handles only the lower bound.
- **Boundary**: the lower bound is for deterministic protocols in the worst case. Randomized protocols can be faster in expectation, which we will meet in the asynchrony part.
