## Planned coverage

- **The definition of good-case latency**: when the sender is honest, how quickly can honest nodes output.
- **Tight bounds per model**: per the reading list, the original (PODC 2021) categorizes the tight good-case latency bounds under each model. The exact numbers await checking against the original.
- **Use**: as a reference for the later evolution of DAG protocol latency, for example to see how far Mysticeti is from the lower bound.
- **Boundary**: the distinction between the good case and the worst case, and that expected and worst-case must not be mixed.
