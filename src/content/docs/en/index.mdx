<p class="home-intro">Byzantine fault tolerance (BFT) asks how the remaining nodes can still agree when some nodes lie or send arbitrary messages. This site walks from the Byzantine Generals Problem through impossibility results such as FLP and FLM to asynchronous BFT and DAG-BFT. Each protocol and theorem page carries a model card stating whether the result holds under synchrony, partial synchrony or asynchrony.</p>

## The route through this site

First separate the three timing models, then see what each one makes possible. The partial-synchrony and asynchrony lines eventually converge in DAG-BFT.
